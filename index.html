Questions

Before you get in touch

How does a project start?

You send us a short brief. We reply with questions, hold a short discovery call, and then send a written scope, timeline and price for you to approve.

How long does a project take?

It depends on the size. A business website usually takes a few weeks, while a custom platform or mobile app takes a few months. You get a written timeline before we start.

Will I own the source code?

Yes. We hand over the source code, documentation and access to every account we set up for you.

Do you support the product after launch?

Yes. We offer monthly support plans that cover fixes, updates, monitoring and small improvements.

Have an idea or a problem to solve?

Tell us what you need. We will reply with questions, a plan and a clear next step.

About

A lab for building useful software.

Bembex-Lab is an IT company that turns business problems into working products. We are engineers and designers who care about how software actually gets used.

What we do

Many businesses know exactly what they want software to do, but struggle to find a team that will build it well and stay to support it.

Bembex-Lab exists to be that team. We design, build and maintain websites, mobile apps and custom systems, from a single landing page to a full business platform.

We work in the open. You see designs before we build them, working software while we build it, and clear reports after it launches.

What clients can expect

  • A named person to talk to throughout the project
  • Plain-language updates instead of technical noise
  • Testing on real devices and real data
  • Source code and documentation handed to you
  • Honest advice, including when a simpler option is better

Why we call it a lab

A lab is where ideas get tested before they get expensive. We work the same way: we prototype early, test with real users, and change direction while it is still cheap to do so.

  • Clickable prototypes before development begins
  • Short build cycles with a demo at the end of each
  • Automated tests that run on every change
  • Honest feedback on what should be simpler
